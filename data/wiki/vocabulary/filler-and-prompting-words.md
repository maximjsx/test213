**Хайде и Айде**

* **Usage**: To encourage or initiate action.
* **Example**: Хайде да го направим! (Let's do it!) *The more 'formal' version would be Нека да го направим!*

### Ами (and its dialect form Еми)

* **Usage**: To emphasize or confirm something. Can also express hesitation or contemplation when used alone with a pause.

* **Examples**:
  * Ами да! Ами така е! (Confirmation, agreement)
  * Ами да го беше направил, де! (Emphasis, in this case - frustration)
  * Ами.. не знам откъде да започна.. (Hesitation, uncertainty)

### Ох

* **Usage**: Expresses pain, discomfort, or sighing.
* **Example**: Ох, боли ме главата. (Oh, my head hurts.)

### Абе

* **Usage**: Expresses disagreement, astonishment, or attracting attention.
* **Example**: Абе, какво правиш? (Hey, what are you doing?)

### Бе

* **Usage**: Like "abe" but more informal. Used to attract attention or when expressing doubt.
* **Example**: Бе, сигурен ли си? (Are you sure?)

### Леле

* **Usage**: Expresses surprise or admiration.
* **Example**: Леле, колко си пораснал! (Wow, you've grown so much!)

### Виж

* **Usage**: To draw attention to something or to emphasize what is being said.
* **Example**: Виж, това е важно. (Look, this is important.)

### Я

* **Usage**: To invite or attract attention.
* **Example**: Я ела тук! (Hey, come here!)

### Де

* **Usage**: Used to amplify a request or command.
* **Example**: Кажи ми, де! (Tell me, please!)

### Нали

* **Usage**: To confirm or seek consent.
* **Example**: Нали ще дойдеш утре? (You'll come tomorrow, right?)

### Тц

* **Usage**: For denial or disagreement.
* **Example**: Тц, не съм съгласен. (No, I don't agree.)
