The word "няма" means "there is no" or "there isn't", but it can also mean "will not" when talking about the future.

### Examples "will not":

* Няма да бъда гладен тази вечер. (I will not be hungry this evening)
* Няма да прочете книгата. (He/She will not read the book)
* Няма да отиде да работи в Тетевен това лято. (He/She will not go to work in Teteven this summer)
* Няма да успее скоро. (He/She will not succeed soon)

### Examples "there is no"

* Няма вода в бутилката. (There is no water in the bottle)
* Няма време. (There is no time)
* Няма кафе в кухнята. (There is no coffee in the kitchen)

> The expression "не ще […]" also means "will not", but it's rarely used nowadays.

### "няма" vs. "нема"

* **Written**: "няма" (there is no, there are no).
  * Example: "Няма време." (There is no time.)
* **Spoken/Slang**: "нема" is a common colloquial form.
  * Example: "Нема проблем." (No problem.)
