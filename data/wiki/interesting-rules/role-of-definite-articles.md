In Bulgarian, definite articles are important in writing. For masculine nouns, these can be "-ът" or "-а".

For example:

* градът (the city)
* входът (the entrance)

To determine if it's "градът" or "града," you can use a rule: if the noun is the subject of the sentence, use "-ът" (градът е голям - the city is big). If it's an object, use "-а" (виждам града - I see the city).

In writing, using the correct article is essential.

**However**, in speaking, you can just say "==градъ==" instead of worrying about "градът" or "града,".
