* **"ли"** is the neutral question particle.
  * Example: "Идваш ли?" (Are you coming?)
* **"да не би да"** asks with suspicion, worry or surprise, roughly "You're not ..., are you?" or "Could it be that ...?"
  * Example: "Да не би да идваш?" (Don't tell me you're coming?)
  * Example: "Да не би да си болен?" (You're not sick, are you?)
