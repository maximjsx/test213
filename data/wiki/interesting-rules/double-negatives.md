In Bulgarian, a double negative is not a mistake and does not cancel out. Words like **никой** (nobody), **нищо** (nothing), **никъде** (nowhere) and **никога** (never) need **не** or **няма** in the same sentence.

* "Не видях никого." (I didn't see anybody.) Literally "I didn't see nobody."
* "Никой не дойде." (Nobody came.)
* "Не искам нищо." (I don't want anything.)
* "Никога не пуша." (I never smoke.)
* "Няма никой." (There is nobody here.)

You can stack several of them, which is still correct: "Никой никога не ми казва нищо." (Nobody ever tells me anything.)
