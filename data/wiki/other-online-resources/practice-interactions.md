If you want to practice your written or spoken Bulgarian without doing it in person, there are a few ways to do so.

## Find someone online who is willing to help you in exchange for help in your language

For example join this Discord server <https://discord.gg/languagesimp> and find someone who is offering bulgarian in the lang-exchange channel:

 ![](/wiki/other-online-resources-practice-interactions-1.png)

Or write an offer yourself.
